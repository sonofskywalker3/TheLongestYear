# Deja-vu dialogue lines (draft for Jeff's review)

Companion to `2026-08-27-deja-vu-dialogue-design.md`. Tier 1 plays at familiarity 60 to 179
("have we met?"); tier 2 at 180 and up ("I trust you, somehow"). One line per tier per villager to
start; the fallback pool covers anyone without a pool. No line explains the loop. Status: DRAFT,
not coded.

## Fallback pool (any villager without lines below)

Tier 1
- I swear we've met before. Do you have a twin?
- Have we met? You seem so familiar.
- Sorry, I almost called you by name... and I don't think you ever told me it.

Tier 2
- I don't know why, but I feel very comfortable with you.
- You feel like an old friend. Isn't that strange?
- I feel like I can trust you. I couldn't say why.
