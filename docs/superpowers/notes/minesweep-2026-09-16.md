## Reading

- The stone-count and ore side of the mine model is right. Two cells are wrong the way that
  matters for asks: **Coal is far under** (asks come out too easy) and **Quartz and the six
  gems are far over** (asks come out too hard for a player who is not cracking geodes).
- The monster table is right for the common drops every monster in a band gives (slime, bug
  meat, bat wing, essences within reason). For a drop that only one uncommon monster gives, the
  "420 kills of that one monster" assumption is 3 to 25x what clearing floors yields. Whether a
  player is expected to farm one monster is a design call, not a measurement.
