# JP budget per loop — measured 2026-08-21 (v0.12.6, `tly_jpbudget`)

**Method.** Clone of `None_443632257` (a pre-engine save at Run 31), deployed v0.12.6, five
unattended `tly_reset`s (loops 36–40, seeds 1459609979 / -223638142 / 935735692 / 984797686 /
1923258252), `tly_jpbudget` after each. Baseline economy (no `jp_boost` tiers), default
`JpSettings` (1/3/10/25 per rarity; Spring 1×, Summer 1.5×, Fall 2.5×, Winter 4×; bundle 15×,
room 60×, weekly quest 30×, checkpoint 100× at the entering week; vault gold/1000 unscaled).
Raw log: session scratchpad `smapi-jpbudget2.txt`; calculator: `JpBudgetCalculator` (Core,
unit-tested); command: `tly_jpbudget [verbose]` (diagnostics only, nothing persisted).

**Rules the model honours.** A pick-X-of-Y bundle pays for at most X slots (the other Y−X die
on completion); each paid slot pays once (`DonationObserver` passes count=1, so ×99 Wood = ×1
Wood); the selection (weekly-theme 1.5×) bonus is applied "at the cap" — the richest
`4 weeks × {4,5,6,7}` payouts per season; a bundle completes in the season of its X-th
donation, a room when its last bundle does; every week's quest bonus and all three checkpoints
are paid.

Three figures per board:

- **EARLIEST** — donate every slot as soon as it is obtainable (most slots are Spring-obtainable
  → paid at 1×). The floor for a player who "just donates".
- **STRONG** — meet each checkpoint minimum with the cheapest obtainable slots, hoard everything
  else for Winter's 4×. What a player who understands the economy banks.
- **CEILING** — every payable slot and every bonus at 4×; ignores checkpoints (no real strategy
  reaches it).

Under STRONG, **≈ 70 % of the loop's JP lands in Winter** (Winter donations 1,450–2,300 +
selection bonus 700–1,100 + bundle/room completions 2,520–2,640 + 880 fixed). Spring is worth
≈ 300–400 JP to a strong player (≈ 1,600–2,100 to an "everything now" player) — relevant to the
Spring-28 reset-farming cheese (Dusklight7): one Spring farmed to the bone yields at most ~2,000,
and only ~350 if the player also wants a competitive Winter.

## Side findings (not fixed here)

- `BundleCatalogBuilder.Build` catalogs only the **first X** ingredients of a pick-X-of-Y bundle
  (`take = Math.Min(NumberOfSlots, Ingredients.Count)`), so 12–28 board ids per loop (Wine, Caviar,
  Duck Egg, Ancient Fruit, Prismatic Shard, the tree fruits, Oak Resin/Pine Tar, Powdermelon, rings,
  Rusty Sword…) have no rarity/season entry → the weekly-theme sampler treats them as Common +
  year-round. Likely a contributor to ada113's "season goals loaded partial items" (C7 audit).
- `SeasonResolver` prefers the crop season over forage, so Grape (Summer forage, Fall crop) reads
  Fall-only → excluded from Summer weekly goals; the gate itself is unaffected (C7 audit).
- `The Missing` (Abandoned Joja Mart) is never classified (room has no theme) — pays donation JP
  but gates nothing.
- `jp_boost_5` (+25 %) would lift STRONG to ≈ 10,000–11,900.
