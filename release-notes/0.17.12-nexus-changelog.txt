0.17.12 - Quest scenes, the Rusty Key, the Copper Pan and Willy's letter come back

Fixed
- Willy's back-room letter arrives after you finish the Community Center and keep playing. A
  finished hall could still be read as unfinished by the mod's own check, which silently blocked
  the game from sending the letter, so Ginger Island stayed shut. The restored hall is now the
  authority. Reported by ChaoticMindset.
- Jodi's fish casserole dinner and Marnie's cave carrot thank-you play again each loop. They
  were treated as already seen from your first loop, so the quest came back but could never be
  turned in. Reported by ChaoticMindset.
- Gunther brings the Rusty Key again each loop. The museum empties on the rewind and the
  60-donation reward re-fired, but his farm visit that hands over the key never played again,
  so the sewers stayed shut after loop 1 unless you had bought the keep. Reported by
  ChaoticMindset.
- Willy's Copper Pan scene, and any scene that only unlocks after another scene that replays
  (the 14-heart chains, Shane's morning-after, Pam's bus follow-up), play again each loop. Found
  by sweeping every vanilla scene for the same fault.
- (0.17.8) A must-donate-all bundle's season gate takes any of its items; the pins only decide
  how many are due. Reported by ada113.
- (0.17.8) The Cookbook and Craftbook open between the shrine and the reset, so a book bought at
  the loop-boundary shrine has something to bank. Reported by ada113.

Changed
- tly_runstate prints the Community Center completion check as Willy's back-room letter reads
  it, for diagnosing a keep-playing save that never got the letter.

Full changelog: https://github.com/sonofskywalker3/TheLongestYear/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md
