Season pity: fails are counted per season; the first five at a season are standard difficulty. From the sixth fail at the same season, after the keep-or-reshuffle question, the Junimos offer to make the town's requests easier: a kept board's quota for that season comes down 10% per extra fail (floor 50%); a reshuffled board leaves out the hardest items eligible for that season (two per extra fail, quality asks first). Say no for a standard board. Accepting is free the first time, then 50/100/200/300 JP in a row (config PityCosts); declining resets the price. Passing the season drops its count back to five. TLY Custom boards only.
No quality asks on items that cannot carry quality (gold Fiber, gold River Jelly, silver Tea Leaves reported in 0.12.16): the engine derives from the game's data which items receive quality (crop harvests, rod-caught fish, spawned forage) and only asks for silver or gold on those.
Pierre's year-2 crops (Garlic, Red Cabbage, Artichoke) stay out of every bundle and weekly goal until you own the shrine upgrade that lets you grow them in year 1.
Keep Pet keeps every pet, not just the first one found.
Traveling Cart cap is per day: buying an item leaves the slot empty until the next visit instead of pulling the next item into view; the Cart Whisperer preview matches.
