0.17.1 - The Dye bundle never asks for a legendary fish

Fixed
- The Dye bundle picks by colour from every object, so a green Legend or a red Crimsonfish was a
  legal pick and an Extreme board wanted both. It no longer draws legendaries at all. Its season
  gates were already right: a bundle can never ask for an item before the season it first exists
  in, so red Cranberries stay a Fall ask.

Full changelog: https://github.com/sonofskywalker3/TheLongestYear/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md
