0.19.116 - Focus cards, a double JP goal, and fixes

Covers everything since 0.19.97.

Added
- Focus cards. At the start of every loop the Junimos offer three hands of cards in turn: tools, buildings and knacks. You pick one card from each hand. A picked card can be bought with JP any time that year, the Junimos deliver it, and you keep it for later loops.
- A tool card upgrades your tool where it sits, in the bag or in a chest, with its enchantments, bait and tackle. A tool waiting at Clint's comes straight back at the card's tier, and a tool you don't have yet is handed to you.
- A building card builds a coop, barn, silo or fish pond for free and finished on the spot, or upgrades your coop or barn at once. The shortcuts open at once, and a kitchen or basement is finished overnight. The JP is only taken once the building is placed, so backing out costs nothing.
- A knack card works from the moment you buy it, and a backpack card grows your bag on the spot.
- A Focus tab on the farm statue lists your three cards with their price and a Buy button. The cards also show on the planning hub and as three small icons under the JP box: a card you can buy glows, a bought card is faded.
- A card you don't buy stays at the top of its tab in the upgrade menu when the year rewinds, marked "Your focus", at the same price.
- A goal the Junimos especially want pays double JP. Each theme card can hold one, marked with an x2 badge in the planning hub and on the bundle page, and "(double JP)" on the weekly quest. Every item you donate to it earns twice the usual JP.
- Saves from before focus cards get their picks at the next planning hub.

Changed
- A Cart Stall slot bought mid-day opens on the Traveling Cart that same day.
- Focus card picks on a controller: A picks the card you are pointing at, and holding a button can't pick on the next hand.

Fixed
- The Keep tool offer finds your best tool wherever it is: in the backpack, at Clint's counter, or in any chest, fridge or Junimo chest on the farm. A spare basic pickaxe no longer hides your iridium one, and a tool at Clint's no longer drops out of the offer.
- With Random pairings on, a card's drawback can no longer shut the only way to get one of that week's goals (for example "mines closed" with a Cave Carrot goal). It draws a different drawback instead.
- After a rewind with the shortcuts kept, villagers headed for the beach (Elliott, Sebastian, Willy and others) keep their routine. The shortcuts are open from the first morning.
- A rod focus card bought before Willy's beach scene no longer earns a second Bamboo Pole.
