0.17.6 - A crops bundle every season, one jelly a week

Changed
- The Pantry always has Spring Crops, Summer Crops and Fall Crops on the board. A board could
  roll other Pantry bundles in their place, which left the Farming theme with nothing to ask for
  in week 1 but saplings.
- A weekly theme asks for at most one of Sea Jelly, River Jelly and Cave Jelly. Two of them in
  the same week meant two slow, separate fishing trips for one theme.
