0.17.3 - Every farm type, Skip intro is back, new book art

Added
- Every farm type is allowed. The mod no longer forces the Standard farm on new games. Kept
  buildings go back to your own spots and the Junimo stash sits by your door on any map.
- The Skip intro checkbox on the character screen works again. It skips the opening scene and
  goes straight to the theme picker. A note recommends watching it the first time you play.

Changed
- The Cookbook, Craftbook and Bundle Log have proper covers now, drawn by supercam19. Thank you!

Full changelog: https://github.com/sonofskywalker3/TheLongestYear/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md
