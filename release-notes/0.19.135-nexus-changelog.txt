0.19.135 - Artichoke, Garlic and Red Cabbage asks fit what you can grow

Covers everything since 0.19.133.

Fixed
- Without the upgrade that has Pierre sell year-two seeds, Artichoke, Garlic and Red Cabbage only come from Mixed Seeds, but a bundle could ask for dozens. Now bundles, weekly goals, goods made from these crops and dishes that need them ask for at most 2 on Easy, 3 on Normal, 4 on Hard and 5 on Extreme, going by your Stack Size setting.
- Once you own the Pierre upgrade, these crops are asked for like any other crop.
- A board already in play keeps its counts until your next rewind.

Changed
- The Junimo Points earned tooltip now says what each step gives: Easy 1.5x, Normal 1x, Hard 0.75x and Extreme 0.5x. Harder settings give fewer points, so upgrades take more loops to afford.

Full changelog: https://github.com/sonofskywalker3/TheLongestYear/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md
