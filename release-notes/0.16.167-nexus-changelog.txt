0.16.167 - JP Boosts, Gifts of the Junimos, three new themes, a board that counts every slot

First release since 0.16.17 (0.16.18 to 0.16.166 were internal builds).

Added
- JP Boosts. The Junimo statue on your farm now has Active / Boosts / Plan tabs. Boosts sells
  eighteen this-loop buys for JP: Rain Dance, Storm Call, Fortune's Favor, Second Wind (instant);
  Overgrowth, Feeding Frenzy, Growth Spurt, Rich Veins, Windfall, Double Trouble, Full Steam,
  Double Yolk, Quick Feet, Year-Two Seeds (a week); Haggler, Fast Friends, Iron Lungs, Sneak Peek
  (a season); Crash Course (a temporary skill level, price climbs with each one, never to 10,
  never keepable) and Elevator Pass (the next floor ending in 0). Week rows stack with the weekly
  theme. Active shows what is running and when it ends; Plan shows every keep's price and what
  the locked ones still need.
- Gifts of the Junimos. A new shrine category: Keep Greenhouse (put back where you left it),
  Keep Quarry Bridge, Keep Boulder Cleared, Keep Minecarts, Keep Bus Unlocked. Offered once you
  have earned that room's reward this loop; 1,000 JP for the first, +1,000 per Gift owned, up
  to 5,000. The room's bundles stay on the board and still pay.
- Three new weekly themes: Spelunking, Artisan and Kitchen (goals by item kind anywhere on the
  board). The weekly JP bonus is paid goal by goal.
- Keep your wallet items and Stardrops (eighteen keeps), and the Garden Pot recipe.
- A toggle to re-send the Better Start gift each loop (on by default).

Changed
- Donations are tracked per Community Center slot: one deposit credits one bundle, repeated
  items need every slot, and a Winter win needs a truly finished Center.
- Bundles roll from the full pool of their kind, no item asked twice, seasonal forage only in
  its season, Night Fishing only night fish, legendary fish and year-two crops at low odds, and
  every item has a first week it can exist so no gate asks for the unreachable.

Fixed
- Keep Bus Unlocked now runs the bus and opens the desert from day 1 (the Vault bundles stay on
  the board and still pay).
- Heart-event invitations send again every loop, so 8 and 10 heart events are reachable after a
  rewind.
- Keep Horse waits until a stable exists this loop.
- Stardew Valley Expanded saves keep season pity.
- A pending level-up menu no longer survives the rewind.

Full changelog: https://github.com/sonofskywalker3/TheLongestYear/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md
