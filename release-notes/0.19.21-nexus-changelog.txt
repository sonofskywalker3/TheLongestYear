0.19.21 - No Harmony error with ExtraAnimalConfig, and rare pond products no longer date items

Covers everything since 0.19.18.

Fixed
- With ExtraAnimalConfig or a similar animal mod installed, TLY no longer shows a red "Harmony patch failed" error when the game starts. On a Wildcard snow day your animals still stay inside, and the other mod's animal settings keep working. Thanks to pitytheviolins for the report.
- A fish pond product with only a tiny chance no longer decides the earliest week an item can be asked for. With Stardew Valley Expanded, Golden Pumpkin was put at week 5; it now waits for the Spirit's Eve maze in week 12. A pond product counts only when it turns up often enough to get in a run.

Changed
- Items that had only a rare pond product as their route now get their real ones: seeds from wild trees, Coral and Sea Urchins from the tide pools past the beach bridge, geodes from the mines, and the Pearl and Treasure Chest from the Squid Fest.

Full changelog: https://github.com/sonofskywalker3/TheLongestYear/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md
